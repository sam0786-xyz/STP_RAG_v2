# STP-RAG manuscript guidelines

Use a single-column article format for this working draft. The manuscript must
be readable at 10-point type and use one paragraph in the abstract. This is not
a venue-submission template. Before submission, replace it with the official
template, anonymity policy, and page limit of the selected venue.

The central rule is evidence calibration: distinguish reference design from
implemented behavior and from audited pilot observations. Never turn an
aggregate difference into a significant, causal, general, or production claim.
Keep limitations substantive and visible.
